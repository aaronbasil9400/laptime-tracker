# Improvement follow-up 001

Date: 2026-10-25
Scope: all five critique issues, led by faster first-time logging; Code-first.

## Implemented

1. Faster logging: searchable native circuit/car suggestions, up to three recent combinations,
   and session details behind a native disclosure with the saved defaults visible. Session and
   conditions now remain selected after saving. Successful saves return focus to the time field.
2. Correct form states: the hidden attribute takes precedence over button styles. Cancel edit
   is only shown in edit mode and returns to the last saved combination without changing the lap.
3. Trustworthy progression: global summary reports record size. Median change is calculated
   within one game, circuit, car and condition, needs six laps, and explains its early/recent
   windows. Consistency groups separate cars and conditions and require three laps for a bar.
4. Keyboard access: lap context stays visible on desktop and mobile; input focus outlines are
   retained. Comparison buttons open the exact progression and focus its heading.
5. History review: game, circuit, car and session filters, active-filter counts, a clear action,
   recoverable empty results, and comparison-to-progression navigation.
6. Import copy now describes its merge behavior accurately.

## Mobile

- 16px text inputs avoid phone browser focus zoom.
- Circuit and car share a row on phones at least 360px wide; narrower screens stack them.
- Main navigation, game choices and row actions have suitable touch targets; small buttons
  reach 44px on coarse pointers.
- Chart labels have 11px text, stronger contrast and enough left space for full lap times.
- Long notes and car names wrap; recent combinations scroll inside their own strip.
